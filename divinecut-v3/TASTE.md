# B-roll taste profile — Divine Success Flow (starter)

## Fingerprint
- Talking-head business/AI content for Indian + global founder audience.
- B-roll style: receipts (tweets, headlines, dashboards), real product UI,
  clean stock of offices/cities/hands-on-keyboard. Modern, premium, no cheese.
- Cadence: dense in the first 10s (hook support), sparse after. Beats 2-4s.

## Guardrails (bans)
- NO memes unless Yashaswi supplies them himself.
- NO watermarked stock previews, NO burned-in captions from sources.
- NO politician/celebrity footage out of context. NO war/disaster imagery.
- NO synthetic-looking AI stock for literal things that have real footage.

## Preferences
- Audio: strip ALL b-roll audio (voice talks over it).
- Stills: subtle Ken Burns zoom-in ~1.5%/sec (sub-pixel method, zoompan BANNED).
- Attribution: small white credit bottom-right for third-party footage.
- AI-generated b-roll: OFF by default.

## Trusted sources (topic-tagged, grow this list)
- AI/tech: official product channels (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), The Verge
- Business India: ET, Mint, Moneycontrol headlines (as screenshots)
- Stock: Pexels, Mixkit, Coverr (free tiers, no watermark)
